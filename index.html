The Story Behind the Table

Every year my family throws a big get-together for 60+ friends and relatives — and every year the same thing happens: the food runs out before Aunty Priya even makes it through the door.

This year we planned a proper buffet, but life had other plans (plumbing, don't ask). Now our catering budget is stretched thin and our menu is… humble. We're talking 3 dishes when we really need at least 7 to survive the crowd.

"Baba always said a good host feeds people until they beg you to stop. We're not there yet. Help us get there." 🙏

Every rupee you chip in goes directly to the caterer. No middleman, no mystery fees — just more paneer, more biryani, and maybe even a dessert station if we hit the stretch goal.
